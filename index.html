A cruise day

  1. 1

    Wake up at the spot

    The boat is already anchored where the wind is. Breakfast, then you are on the water.

  2. 2

    Kite until the afternoon

    Lessons run on board for everyone who wants them. The rest ride on their own.

  3. 3

    Sail on in the evening

    The boat moves to the next spot while you eat. You wake up somewhere new.

Hi, I'm Bakar

I am a passionate kitesurf instructor from Egypt with deep roots in the Red Sea. I have spent over 14 years on the water, teaching hundreds of students from around the world how to ride, progress and fall in love with kitesurfing.

From first-time beginners to advanced riders chasing new tricks, I have guided students through every step of their journey. My home base is El Gouna, where steady winds and turquoise lagoons make perfect conditions to learn and grow.

For me, kitesurfing is more than technique. It is about sharing the joy, freedom and energy of the ocean.
